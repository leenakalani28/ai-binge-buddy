AI Binge Buddy - Final Button Audit
11 October 2026

RESULTS
25/25 offline button/action cases passed.
6/6 external-link variants used the expected configured fixture URLs.
23/23 existing regression tests passed.
Wheel JavaScript: all three stopping outcomes passed.
Scroll guard: repeat wheel reruns do not replay top scrolling; new navigation tokens do.

LIMITATIONS
TMDB and Groq responses were mocked to avoid quota/network dependence.
No live browser was available. Actual top/middle/bottom/last-position landing was NOT measured.
A rendered scroll command proves intent, not its execution in a browser.
External URLs were checked against test metadata; external pages were not opened.
No application features were changed during this audit.

SCROLL CONCERNS
Group and preference destinations do not consume scroll_top_pending.
Start over clears session state without issuing a home scroll command.
Therefore a correct destination does not prove a top landing. These paths may retain a previous scroll position or be clamped by the browser when page height changes.
No middle or bottom landing has been confirmed by this audit.
